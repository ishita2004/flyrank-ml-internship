## 4. Pressure-Test Prompt & Output

### The Pressure-Test Prompt
> *"This is my proof statement: 'I build Applied Search Intelligence and ML prioritization systems that convert noisy search performance data into evidence-backed, actionable editorial queues. I am proving this to an ML Engineering Hiring Manager, so they will email me to schedule a technical chat.'*  
> *This is my sitemap: Homepage (/), Case Studies (/work), About & Philosophy (/about), Contact (/contact).*  
> *Does this sitemap walk my one person from landing, to believing me, to taking my one action? Tell me which pages earn their place, which are wasted, and what is missing. Be specific, not encouraging."*

### AI Response Summary
> **Evaluation:**  
> - **Homepage (`/`)**: Earns its place, but must show proof immediately above the fold—don't hide results behind clicks.  
> - **Case Studies (`/work`)**: Essential for technical hiring managers. Make sure the methodology, leakage checks, and metrics are visible without downloading code.  
> - **About (`/about`)**: Borderline. Hiring managers care more about problem-solving framework than personal bio.  
> - **Contact (`/contact`)**: A separate `/contact` page is a friction point. If a visitor spends 20 seconds, forcing them to click a separate contact link drops conversion.

## 5. Noted Change Based on Pressure-Test

> **Key Improvement Made:**  
> I will **collapse the separate `/contact` page into a persistent, sticky CTA button and footer on every single page**, and **embed a 2-card summary of Case Study 1 directly on the Homepage**. This ensures a hiring manager sees real proof within 10 seconds of landing and can take the primary action instantly without navigating away.
